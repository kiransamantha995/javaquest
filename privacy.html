Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Last updated: November 24, 2026. This Privacy Policy details how JavaQst ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards user information across our web platform and mobile app.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection necessary to provide a personalized coding experience:

  • Account Details: When creating an account, we collect your email address and custom player username via Supabase Authentication. Guests can access the app without submitting personal details.
  • Educational Progress: Completed lessons, earned XP, streak history, badges, and capstone project code are saved to synchronize your learning state across devices.
  • AI Tutor Interactions: Student queries sent to Meowger are processed transiently via our Google Gemini backend proxy. We do not sell or monetize student conversations.

2. Data Security & Encryption

All data transmitted between your device and JavaQst is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 (HTTPS). Cloud databases are hosted on enterprise-grade Supabase PostgreSQL clusters with row-level security (RLS) enforcement.

3. Right to Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Store policies and international data privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA), users have the right to permanently erase their account and all associated records at any time. You can trigger immediate account deletion directly within the in-app Settings > Delete Account screen, or by contacting our data protection officer at support@javaqst.in.

4. Children's Privacy

JavaQst is an educational tool suitable for students of all ages. We do not knowingly harvest personally identifiable information from children under 13 without parental consent.
